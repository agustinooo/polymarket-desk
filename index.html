caveats

  • Price-history points are last-trade/mid snapshots, not executable asks; real fills would be worse in thin buckets.
  • Survivorship/selection: only markets Gamma still lists as closed+resolved in the tag feeds; capped random samples per type.
  • Weather 'observed' values for the error model come from the winning bucket (mid of bucket), not raw station data.
  • Short window (days) and correlated samples (buckets in the same event, assets moving together) make P&L noisy.
  • Open-Meteo previous-run leads are chosen so every hour of the obs day was forecast >= 6h before t.
  • Samples are dropped when the market was <6h old, had <3 price points in the prior 6h, had never moved, or decision-time volume (USDC traded in the window before t, from data-api trades) below BT_MIN_DECISION_VOL. Lifetime volume is NOT used by default: it is measured after resolution and winners attract volume (lookahead).
  • historical CLOB price-history point at t + 0.01 half-spread, plus market taker fee
  • Not modelled: order-book depth/slippage beyond half-spread, min order size, liquidity filter, partial fills
